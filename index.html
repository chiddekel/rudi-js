How does learning with Rudi work?
Instead of an 800-page compilers textbook: short lessons where you touch every mechanism.

      

        
Step 1
Learn 5 minutes a day
Short lessons and quizzes. A mistake costs a heart, and the exercise returns at the end until you master it.

        
Step 2
Arrange tokens and AST trees
Split code into tokens, arrange nodes from the root, predict precedence and bytecode.

        
Step 3
Run it in the Playground
A real mini-engine in the browser: tokens, AST, bytecode and a live console.
